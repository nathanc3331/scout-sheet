Rules used

Numbers run 1 to 1000 and wrap, so 10 is 10 away from both 20 and 1000. The difference (0 to 500) lands in stacked ranges: HR, 3B, 2B, 1B, BB, FO, K, PO, RGO, LGO.

Range sizes are batter type plus pitcher type (501 points), plus the pitcher's hand bonus when hands match, then park factors and infield in. Values come from the Type Listings in the MLR rulebook (rev. Sep 14, 2026).

A difference of 496 to 500 can become a lineout or triple play with runners on; the sheet scores it as the LGO it would otherwise be.
